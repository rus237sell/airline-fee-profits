# Sources

All figures are published totals, not estimates, unless labeled as a
counterfactual in the script.

## BTS annual releases (U.S. scheduled passenger airlines)

- 2019: BTS, "2019 Annual and 4th Quarter U.S. Airline Financial Data"
  (May 4, 2020). Revenue $196.2B, operating profit $20.8B, net $14.8B,
  baggage fees $5.8B, reservation change fees $2.8B, 21 airlines.
  https://www.bts.gov/newsroom/2019-annual-and-4th-quarter-us-airline-financial-data
- 2020: BTS 30-21 (May 3, 2021). Revenue $77.3B, operating loss $46.5B,
  net loss $35.0B, baggage fees $2.8B, change fees $0.9B ($898M), 23 airlines.
  https://www.bts.gov/newsroom/us-airlines-2020-net-profit-down-35-billion-2019
- 2023: BTS 24-24 (May 15, 2024). Revenue $236.3B, operating profit $13.2B,
  net $7.8B, baggage fees $7.1B, change fees $1.1B, 26 airlines.
  https://www.bts.gov/newsroom/us-airlines-gain-78-billion-2023-increase-over-2022
- 2024: BTS 28-25. Revenue $247.2B, operating profit $13.5B, net $6.7B,
  baggage fees $7.3B, change fees $1.0B, 25 airlines.
  https://www.BTS.gov/newsroom/us-airlines-net-profit-was-67-billion-2024-decrease-over-2023
- 2025: BTS 26-43 (May 12, 2026). Revenue $252.6B, operating profit $11.4B,
  net $6.0B, baggage fees $7.4B, change fees $1.1B, 25 airlines.
  https://www.bts.gov/newsroom/us-airlines-profited-60-billion-2025-decrease-over-2024

BTS note carried into the analysis: baggage fees and reservation change
fees are the only passenger ancillary fees BTS reports as separate items.
Revenue from seat assignments and onboard sales is reported in other
categories and cannot be identified separately.

## SEC EDGAR

- Company facts API (data.sec.gov), 10-K fiscal-year frames for Delta
  (CIK 27904), United (CIK 100517), American (CIK 6201), and Southwest
  (CIK 92380): RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax and
  OperatingIncomeLoss, pulled live by the script at run time.
